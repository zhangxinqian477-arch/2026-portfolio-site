# Map data attribution

The globe texture is generated from Natural Earth 1:110m map data, redistributed by the `world-atlas` package. Natural Earth data is in the public domain.

- Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- world-atlas: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- Texture projection generated with d3-geo and topojson-client.
